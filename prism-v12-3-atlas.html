Key Innovation in v12.3: Archetypes now use calibrated position (1-5 scale) and salience (0-3) values for all 15 dimensions. This enables precise archetype matching and reveals the full trait signature of each political type.

Notation: L/M/H = position (1-2=Low, 3=Mid, 4-5=High). + = high salience (2-3), ~ = mid salience (1). Salience 0 = trait not shown. Example: MAT:L+ means Material position left (1-2) with high salience.

Anti-traits: Some archetypes are defined by opposition to a position (marked with "anti" in source data). These represent active rejection rather than passive low salience.

Notable Examples: Public figures listed as illustrative archetypes. Year ranges indicate when someone fit this archetype. † = historical figure.
