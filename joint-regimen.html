四种补充剂 — The Four Supplements

1. Glucosamine Chondroitin MSM with OptiMSM
氨基葡萄糖 + 软骨素 + MSM（关节修复三合一）
Glucosamine 氨基葡萄糖 Chondroitin 软骨素 MSM 甲基磺酰甲烷
What it does: The foundation of the stack. Glucosamine is a building block your body uses to make and repair cartilage — the cushioning between joints. Chondroitin helps cartilage retain water and elasticity, keeping it spongy and shock-absorbent. MSM (OptiMSM is the purest form) is an organic sulfur compound that reduces inflammation and supports connective tissue repair.

作用：氨基葡萄糖是软骨的修复原料，软骨素帮助软骨保持弹性和水分，MSM减少关节炎症，支持结缔组织修复。三者协同是关节保健的基础。
2. BioAvailable Boswellia + UC-II
乳香提取物 + UC-II型胶原蛋白（抗炎 + 免疫调节）
Boswellia 乳香 UC-II 非变性II型胶原蛋白
What it does: Boswellia (Indian frankincense) is a natural anti-inflammatory — it blocks the 5-LOX enzyme pathway, reducing joint swelling and pain without the side effects of NSAIDs. UC-II is undenatured type-II collagen: a small daily dose trains your immune system to stop attacking your own cartilage (oral tolerance mechanism). Clinical studies show 40mg UC-II outperforms glucosamine+chondroitin alone for knee function.

作用：乳香是天然抗炎成分，阻断5-LOX酶通路，减少关节肿胀。UC-II是非变性II型胶原蛋白，每天小剂量可以"训练"免疫系统不再攻击自身软骨。临床研究显示效果优于单独使用氨基葡萄糖。
3. NAC (N-Acetyl Cysteine) 600mg with Selenium
N-乙酰半胱氨酸 + 硒（抗氧化 + 全身保健）
NAC N-乙酰半胱氨酸 Selenium 硒
What it does: NAC is the precursor to glutathione — your body's most powerful antioxidant. It is primarily for general well-being, not joints specifically. However, it supports the joint stack indirectly: chronic joint inflammation generates oxidative stress, which depletes glutathione. NAC replenishes it, helping your body manage the inflammation cycle. Selenium boosts immune function and thyroid health.

作用：NAC是谷胱甘肽（身体最强抗氧化剂）的前体。主要用于全身保健，不是专门针对关节。但慢性关节炎症会产生氧化应激，消耗抗氧化物——NAC帮助补充，间接支持关节恢复。硒增强免疫力。

Glucosamine 氨基葡萄糖 MSM Arnica 山金车 Liposome 脂质体
