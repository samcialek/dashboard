Key Structural Insights

The Nader-Sanders Distinction: Both have similar ENDS positions (MAT:L+, ZS:H+), but Nader is MEANS-terminal (PRO is the point; archetype M01) while Sanders is ENDS-terminal (redistribution is the point; archetype 011). This distinction is captured by PRO salience and position.

The Silver-Nader Distinction: Both have high EPS (Empiricist), but Silver's epistemology is instrumental (good method → good outcomes; archetype 016) while Nader's proceduralism is terminal (fair process IS the goal; archetype M01). Silver is ENDS-primary with EPS modifier; Nader is MEANS-primary.

ONT Splits: Same ENDS/MEANS positions can produce very different political animals based on ontological optimism/pessimism. Revolutionary Progressive (O01) vs. Cynical Progressive (O02) have identical values but opposite theories of change.

REALITY-Primary: ~10% of population has no coherent ENDS positions. They vote AGAINST (elites, decline, the system) not FOR (policy positions). ZS and ONT are load-bearing; activated by candidates/moments rather than ideology.
