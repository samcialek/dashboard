Questions for Matt Hauer — Tuesday March 17 (Wanda's Tavern)

Context: Drinks/casual, not an interview. Matt is Head of Agent Development at Aaru, FSU professor, Cozzarelli Prize winner. You attended their standup, walked the neighborhood with Yundak, saw John live-code. Work trial starts ~April 1. Goal: extract intel that lets you prepare so well it's almost unfair.

Strategy: Don't interrogate. Let conversation flow. Seed topics, let Matt talk. He's an academic — he likes explaining things. Buy him a drink and let him riff.

Tier 1: High-Priority Intel (Must Get)

Tooling & Environment

  1. What IDE does the team use? VS Code? Cursor? JetBrains? Looked like John was using Cursor during the office visit — confirm. Whole team on Cursor, or mixed? Any shared config/extensions?
  2. What's the dev environment setup process? When a new engineer joins, what does Day 1 look like? Is there a setup script, a README, a buddy system? How long until someone ships their first PR?
  3. Git workflow: Stacked PRs (saw Matt doing this), conventional commits, branch naming? Monorepo or multi-repo?
  4. Beyond Linear/Notion/Slack — what else? Figma? Vercel? AWS/GCP? Any internal tools that are homegrown?
  5. CI/CD setup? GitHub Actions? What does the deploy flow look like?
  6. Local dev environment: Docker, virtual envs, or bare metal?
  7. Python environment: Poetry, pip, conda? Python version? Any strong opinions on typing/linting (mypy, ruff)?

Tech Stack (So You Can Practice)

  1. Python/TypeScript split: Is the simulation engine Python and the product layer TypeScript? What's the ratio?
  2. LLM providers: OpenAI, Anthropic, open-source models? Mix of all three?
  3. Databases: How do they store/query synthetic population data at scale? Postgres? ClickHouse for analytics — what's the primary store?
  4. Frameworks: FastAPI confirmed for backend — Next.js for frontend? Anything custom?

The Work Trial Itself

  1. What should I actually expect? Is there a defined project/task, or is it "here's a codebase, find something useful to do"? Who will I report to / pair with?
  2. What would make someone crush a work trial vs. just pass it? What separates "this person is good" from "we need to hire this person immediately"?
  3. What's the biggest gap on the team right now? Where is there work piling up that nobody has bandwidth for?

Architecture & Current Problems

  1. How's the logical consistency module going? Last time you were putting out "little embers." Did the approach land, or did it need a rethink?
  2. What does the Audience Gen pipeline look like end-to-end? Input → trait generation → validation → output. Where are the bottlenecks?
  3. The manuscript — "I'd love to read it before I start. Can you send it to me?" (He offered during the standup visit. Follow through.)

Tier 2: Strategic Intel (Get If Conversation Goes There)

Technical Depth

  1. How do you think about agent fidelity vs. compute cost? At what point do you stop adding complexity to an agent? Is there a target "resolution" for different use cases?
  2. Second-gen tenement traits — what changed from first gen? What was wrong/missing?
  3. How are you validating agent populations against ground truth? The EY study used Spearman correlation. Is that still the standard? What metrics does the team trust?
  4. What's the hardest thing about the classical ML layer? John described 3 stages (off-shelf LLM → fine-tuned → classical ML hybrid). Where does the classical ML part break?
  5. Cross-sectional → longitudinal: Is anyone working on rolling simulations forward in time, or is that still aspirational?

Team & Culture

  1. What does a typical week look like for an engineer? Standup cadence, how much is solo vs. pairing, how much is meetings vs. heads-down?
  2. How does work get assigned? Top-down from Cameron/John, or do people pick from a backlog? How autonomous are engineers?
  3. What's the code review culture like? Fast approvals, or deep review? Who reviews whose code?
  4. How does the team handle disagreements about approach? When Matt the academic and John the systems builder disagree on methodology, how does that resolve?

Product & Clients

  1. What kind of client work would I touch? Is the trial focused on internal tooling, or would I be near client-facing work?
  2. What happened with the 2024 election prediction? Harris 53-47 vs. reality. Was there a post-mortem? What did the team learn?
  3. Mississippi midterms — is that still the next political test case? What's the approach?

Your Angle

  1. Where do you see the biggest methodological gap? As an FSU demographer looking at this from the academic side, what's the thing that keeps you up at night about their approach?
  2. How does the team think about theory-driven vs. data-driven archetypes? You've been doing automated clustering — has anyone proposed using structured priors from social science theory? (This is the PRISM pitch without calling it PRISM.)

  • Don't trash their 2024 election miss. Ask about it with genuine curiosity, not "here's what you did wrong."
  • Don't monopolize the conversation. He's a professor — he has interesting things to say. Let him talk 60% of the time.
