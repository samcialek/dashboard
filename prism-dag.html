L1 — Innate Temperament

6 neurobiological primitives. Largely heritable, stable by late adolescence. TR (Threat Reactivity), ND (Novelty Drive), EMP (Empathic Capacity), RX (Temperamental Reactance), AF (Affiliative Drive), CF (Cognitive Flexibility).

[F] — Formative Refraction MICRO

Family, region, religion, education, class, trauma, media environment. Refracts innate temperament into crystallized personality. Many-to-many mapping. Unobservable per individual — but distributions are estimable from census/survey data at the population level. Re-opens during phase transitions (divorce, war, migration).

L2 — Crystallized Personality

6 dispositional traits. Each is f(2-4 L1 nodes) × [F]. Traditionalism, Economic Conservatism, Compassion, Defiance, Gregariousness, Risk Comfort. Durable but not immutable.

[M] — Milieu MICRO + MACRO

Micro: Personal life events (layoff, divorce, health crisis) — unobservable per individual, but base rates are predictable in aggregate (you can't predict WHO gets divorced, but you can predict the divorce rate). Macro: Recession, inflation, institutional decay — observable, same for everyone. This dual nature makes L3 partly idiosyncratic.

L3 — Political Disposition

14 nodes across 4 clusters. ENDS: MAT, CD, CU, MOR (what you want). MEANS: PRO, COM, EPS*, AES* (how you get there). REALITY: ZS, ONT_H, ONT_S (what you believe is true). SELF: PF, TRB, ENG (who you are politically). 12 continuous (pos 1-5 + salience) + 2 categorical* (6 categories each). The inflection point: last layer before everything becomes macro.

[CA] — Choice Architecture MACRO

Candidates, platforms, ballot access, mobilization, social cost, election cycle, scandal, crisis, media environment. Purely macro — same for everyone in a given district. Fully observable and modelable. This is why L4 (behavior) is more predictable than L3 (disposition): the refraction layer feeding it is entirely observable.

L4 — Political Behavior

Observable output: TURNOUT (binary), VOTE (party choice), RCS (−3 to +3 resistance-compliance scale for non-electoral behavior). NOT a lookup table — f(L3, practical constraints).
