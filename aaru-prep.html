Technical Challenges They Told You About
  1. Cross-sectional vs. longitudinal: Most client work is cross-sectional ("a snapshot"). Rolling simulations forward is aspirational but not current.
  2. Interaction complexity: "The hardest thing for us is dealing with interactions and regressive components" — agent-agent effects create computational explosion.
  3. Markov assumption limitations: First-order Markov misses long-range dependencies ("decisions you made 20 years ago might affect today").
  4. Environment vs. stimulus: Formally separating environment from agent is unsolved.
  5. PCA for scenario reduction: PCA's objective (variance) may not align with causal importance.
  6. Butterfly effect: Small parameter changes → wildly different outcomes. Reproducibility challenge.
  7. Agent inconsistencies: Logical consistency in generated agents is an active problem.
  8. Agents generated behind the script: Bug in the pipeline.
  9. Topological sort for DAGs: Works for acyclic computation, but cycles (agent interactions) break this.
