Architecture Notes

  • Parallel Roots: L1 and [F] are both root nodes (no parents). Neither causes the other.
  • Interaction Effect: L3 is produced by the interaction of L1 × [F], not just their sum. Same trait + different context = different disposition.
  • L1 Fundamentals: T (Threat Sensitivity), EC (Epistemic Curiosity), D (Defiance), G (Group Identification), COMP (Compassion), RC (Reality Calibration)
  • Computed L3 Nodes: RISK_TOL and TIME_HOR are derived from specific L1 × [F] combinations, not primitives.
  • CAT-6 Variables: MOR, EPS, AES, H, TRB are nominal (distinct unordered categories), not ordinal scales.
  • Position + Salience: Every L3 node has two components. Context [C] primarily affects salience (activation), not position.
  • L5 Electoral Branch: ENG sets participation floor. If any candidate's FIT_SCORE exceeds floor, vote for argmax. Otherwise abstain.
  • L5 Non-Electoral Branch: RCS (Regime Compliance Scale) ranges from -3 (Active Resistance) to +3 (Regime Collaboration).
  • One-Shot Model: Assumes single time-point prediction. No L5 → L3 feedback loop modeled.
