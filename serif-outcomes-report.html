Data Collection

Wearable devices: Fitbit (83%), Apple Watch (32%). Continuous streaming of heart rate, HRV, sleep stages, and activity metrics.

Blood panels: Comprehensive metabolic and cardiovascular panels at Day 0 (baseline) and Day 300 (follow-up). Fasting specimens collected at certified laboratories.

Study Protocol

All participants received Serif's personalized recommendations via mobile app. Recommendations updated daily based on wearable data streams. No standardized intervention protocol—Serif's engine tailored guidance to individual response patterns detected during the calibration period (Days 1-14).

Statistical Approach

Primary: Mixed-effects models with Bayesian partial pooling across cohorts. Random intercepts per participant, fixed effects for time and cohort.
Population: Intent-to-treat with last observation carried forward (LOCF) for dropouts.

Statistical Methods

Methodology

Primary Analysis

Model: Mixed-effects linear models with random intercepts per participant, fixed effects for time (Day 0 vs Day 300) and cohort (1, 2, 3).

Correction: Bonferroni correction for multiple comparisons across 4 primary outcomes (α = 0.05/4 = 0.0125).

Population: Intent-to-treat with last observation carried forward (LOCF) for participants lost to follow-up (11.6%).

Effect Size: Cohen's d calculated as standardized mean difference with pooled standard deviation.

Sensitivity Analyses

Per-protocol: Analysis restricted to completers (n = 1,050) yielded consistent effect magnitudes.

ANCOVA: Baseline-adjusted analysis controlling for Day 0 biomarker values.

Regression to mean: Correction applied; effects remained significant.

Outlier exclusion: Participants ±3 SD from mean excluded; results unchanged.

Subgroups: Age (<50/≥50), sex, BMI (<25/25-30/>30) showed consistent direction of effects.
