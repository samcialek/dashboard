Historical Context

The 1983 National Capital Marathon was one of Canada's premier road races, attracting 3,224 finishers. Christopher traveled from Schenectady, New York to compete.

  • 2:46:54 in 1983 was an exceptional amateur time, well within BQ standards
  • Finishing 126th of 3,224 placed him in the top 3.9% of the field
  • His 6:23/mi pace was sustained for the full 26.2 miles
  • The 1983 Boston Marathon qualifying time for men was approximately 2:50:00 — Christopher beat that by over 3 minutes
  • Christopher also served on the race committee for the Mohawk Hudson River Marathon, helping build the Capital Region's running community

The HMRRC Connection

Both Grace and Christopher were active members of the Hudson-Mohawk Road Runners Club (HMRRC), one of the oldest and most respected running clubs in New York's Capital Region. Grace's results span nearly three decades of competition, from winning the 18.9-miler in 1986 to taking female titles into the 2010s. Most of her detailed race records live in the HMRRC Pace Setter newsletter archives, predating the era of digital timing chips.

Like Father, Like Son

Sam's best marathon (3:16:40 at Run for the Red, 2013) puts him about 30 minutes behind his father's 1983 Ottawa time. But the apple doesn't fall far: both finished in the top percentiles of their respective fields, and both chose hilly, out-of-town courses for their biggest races. Christopher's 6:23 pace would have had him crossing the finish line at Stroudsburg a full 30 minutes before Sam — but give Sam 30 more years of training...
