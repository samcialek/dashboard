CORE Agent Consistency & Believability

"How do you know when an agent is lying to you?"

ARCHITECTURE Population Architecture

ARCHITECTURE The Mean/Median/Mode Question

"When you run a simulation — what are you optimizing for? The mean outcome, the median, or the mode?"

"Does it change by product? Polls = modal, Lumen = expected value?"
"Do clients know the difference?"
"Do you show the full distribution or just the point estimate?"
