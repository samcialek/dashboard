PRISM L1 → L2 Mapping

Many-to-Many: Each L2 trait is a product of multiple L1 innate temperaments, refracted through [F] formative context

L1 — Innate Temperament (at birth)

TR Threat Reactivity
Amygdala firing speed. Startle reflex, baseline anxiety. Pure hardware.
ND Novelty Drive
Dopamine-seeking. Craves new stimuli. The toddler who wanders vs. clings.
EMP Empathic Capacity
Mirror neuron / oxytocin sensitivity. How strongly you feel others' emotions.
RX Temperamental Reactance
Oppositional energy. The 2-year-old who says "no" to everything.
AF Affiliative Drive
Need to belong. Separation anxiety, attachment style. Social bonding hardware.
CF Cognitive Flexibility
Ease of updating mental models. Working memory, abstract reasoning.

L2 — Crystallized Personality (~age 18) — Detail

T — Threat Sensitivity

T = f(TR↑, ND, AF) × [F]
Primary: TR (amygdala reactivity). High AF amplifies threat into tribal defensiveness. High ND can dampen it by making novelty exciting rather than scary.
High TR + safe suburb [F]: Mild conservatism, prudent risk management
High TR + war-zone childhood [F]: Hypervigilance, fortress mentality

EC — Epistemic Curiosity

EC = f(ND↑, CF↑, TR↓) × [F]
Requires novelty drive AND cognitive flexibility. High TR suppresses it — new information feels threatening, so you avoid it. Two primary drivers.
High ND + High CF + Low TR: Voracious reader, intellectual explorer
High ND + Low CF + High TR: Thrill-seeker, not an intellectual — seeks sensation, not ideas

COMP — Compassion

COMP = f(EMP↑, AF, CF) × [F]
Primary: empathic capacity. AF determines warmth/bonding energy. CF determines breadth — can you extend empathy beyond your immediate circle?
High EMP + High CF + diverse community [F]: Universal compassion, broad moral circle
High EMP + Low CF + insular community [F]: Deep compassion, but only for "your people"

D — Defiance

D = f(RX↑, TR↓, AF↓) × [F]
Primary: temperamental reactance. But high TR suppresses it (fear beats rebellion) and high AF suppresses it (belonging beats independence). Defiance requires courage AND independence.
High RX + Low TR + Low AF: True rebel, anti-authoritarian, punk energy
High RX + High TR + High AF: Frustrated conformist — wants to rebel but too scared and too attached

G — Group Identification

G = f(AF↑, TR, EMP) × [F]
Primary: affiliative drive. TR makes it tribal/defensive (us vs. them). EMP can broaden it (feeling for outgroup members). [F] determines WHICH group.
High AF + High TR + low EMP: Fierce tribal loyalty, "my people right or wrong"
High AF + Low TR + High EMP: Cosmopolitan belonger — identifies broadly with humanity

RC — Reality Calibration

RC = f(CF↑, TR↓, ND) × [F]
Primary: cognitive flexibility. High TR makes disconfirming evidence feel threatening — you resist updating. ND drives information-seeking that enables calibration.
High CF + Low TR + good education [F]: Strong calibration, updates beliefs on evidence
High CF + High TR + propaganda environment [F]: Smart but defensive — uses intelligence to rationalize, not calibrate

Key Patterns in the Wiring

  • TR (Threat Reactivity) is the most connected L1 node — it's a primary driver of T, an inhibitor of EC, D, and RC. High TR is the single biggest constraint on the personality system.
  • CF (Cognitive Flexibility) is the second most connected — primary for EC and RC, modulator for COMP. It's the "processing power" that enables higher-order traits.
  • AF (Affiliative Drive) appears in 3 L2s but plays different roles each time — amplifies T (tribal threat), suppresses D (belonging over rebellion), and drives G (bonding target).
  • No L2 has fewer than 2 L1 inputs. There are no one-to-one mappings. Every crystallized trait is a product of interacting temperaments.
"L1 is the same across identical twins raised apart. L2 is where they diverge."
The many-to-many mapping means the SAME high-TR infant can become a cautious conservative OR a hypervigilant radical, depending on which other L1 traits are active and what [F] formative context they encountered.
