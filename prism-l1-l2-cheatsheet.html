L1 Innate→ [F] →L2 Crystallized

How formative experience refracts raw temperament into personality
L1 — INNATE (birth)
[F] REFRACTION
L2 — CRYSTALLIZED (~18)
TR
Threat Reactivity
How fast your amygdala fires. Startle reflex, baseline anxiety. Pure hardware.
T
Threat Sensitivity
Calibrated by experience. A high-TR kid in a safe suburb → moderate T. Same kid in a war zone → extreme T.
High TR + safe environment → healthy caution. High TR + abuse → hypervigilance.
ND
Novelty Drive
Dopamine-seeking. How much you crave new stimuli. The toddler who wanders vs. the one who clings.
EC
Epistemic Curiosity
Shaped by whether curiosity was rewarded or punished. Access to books, education, intellectual role models.
High ND + library card → voracious learner. High ND + no access → thrill-seeker.
EMP
Empathic Capacity
Mirror neuron / oxytocin sensitivity. How strongly you feel others' emotions.
COMP
Compassion
Shaped by who you learned to empathize with. Broad (universalist) vs. narrow (in-group only).
High EMP + diverse community → universal compassion. High EMP + insular upbringing → fierce tribal loyalty.
RX
Temperamental Reactance
Oppositional energy. The 2-year-old who says "no" to everything. Innate resistance to being told what to do.
D
Defiance
Shaped by authority experiences. Strict parents might amplify OR suppress it. Trauma can flip it.
High RX + authoritarian home → rebel or broken compliance. High RX + respectful parenting → principled dissent.
AF
Affiliative Drive
How much you need to belong. Separation anxiety, attachment style. Social bonding hardware.
G
Group Identification
Shaped by which groups you were sorted into. Race, religion, class, geography. The drive is innate; the target is formative.
High AF + church community → devout identity. High AF + gang territory → gang loyalty. Same hardware, different flag.
CF
Cognitive Flexibility
How easily you update mental models. Working memory, abstract reasoning. Raw processing.
RC
Reality Calibration
Shaped by epistemological environment. Were you taught to question or to obey? Critical thinking vs. received wisdom.
High CF + Socratic education → rigorous thinker. High CF + dogmatic environment → sophisticated rationalizer.
