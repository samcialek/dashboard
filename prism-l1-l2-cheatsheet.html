L2 NODE DETAILS

T Threat Sensitivity
T = f(TR↑, AF, ND) × [F]
Primary: TR. High AF amplifies tribal/in-group threat detection. High ND can dampen threat sensitivity by making novelty rewarding rather than scary.
High TR + High AF + Low ND
Hypervigilant tribalist. Sees outsiders as existential threats. Watches for danger in every interaction. Fox News grandpa.
High TR + Low AF + High ND
Anxious explorer. Fearful but curious — pushes through anxiety for new experiences. Nervous traveler who goes anyway.
EC Epistemic Curiosity
EC = f(ND↑, CF↑, TR↓) × [F]
Primary: ND + CF. High TR makes new information feel threatening, suppressing curiosity. Need both the drive to seek (ND) and the capacity to process (CF).
High ND + High CF + Low TR
Voracious polymath. Reads everything, changes mind easily, loves being wrong. The scientist archetype.
High ND + Low CF + High TR
Conspiracy theorist. Seeks novelty (ND) but can't update models (low CF) and finds disconfirmation threatening (TR). Collects "evidence" that confirms fears.
COMP Compassion
COMP = f(EMP↑, AF, CF) × [F]
Primary: EMP. AF determines warmth and attachment quality. CF determines breadth — can you extend empathy beyond the familiar? Without CF, compassion stays tribal.
High EMP + High AF + Low CF
Fierce mama bear. Deep empathy for her people, cold to outsiders. Volunteers at church but votes against refugee funding.
High EMP + Low AF + High CF
Abstract humanitarian. Cares about global suffering but feels distant from neighbors. Donates to effective altruism, awkward at barbecues.
D Defiance
D = f(RX↑, TR↓, AF↓) × [F]
Primary: RX. But high TR suppresses it (fear of punishment > rebellion) and high AF suppresses it (need to belong > need for independence). Defiance requires both the impulse AND freedom from fear and conformity pressure.
High RX + Low TR + Low AF
True rebel. Doesn't fear consequences, doesn't need approval. Whistleblower, dropout founder, punk rocker.
High RX + High TR + High AF
Suppressed rebel. Seethes internally but conforms outwardly. Passive-aggressive middle manager. The oppositional energy leaks sideways.
G Group Identification
G = f(AF↑, TR, EMP) × [F]
Primary: AF. TR makes group identity tribal and defensive ("us vs. them"). EMP can broaden it — empathizing with out-group members loosens boundaries.
High AF + High TR + Low EMP
Tribal loyalist. Rides or dies for the in-group, hostile to outsiders. Sports ultras, ethno-nationalists, gang loyalty.
High AF + Low TR + High EMP
Community builder. Deep belonging need but open borders on who counts. Builds diverse coalitions. "The more the merrier."
RC Reality Calibration
RC = f(CF↑, TR↓, ND) × [F]
Primary: CF. High TR makes disconfirming evidence feel threatening — you defend beliefs instead of updating them. ND drives information-seeking that feeds the calibration process.
High CF + Low TR + High ND
Well-calibrated rationalist. Actively seeks disconfirming evidence, updates quickly, comfortable saying "I was wrong."
High CF + High TR + Low ND
Sophisticated rationalizer. Has the horsepower to update but TR makes it painful, and low ND means they don't seek new info. Smart but calcified.
[F] FORMATIVE CONTEXT

The experiences that modulate how L1 inputs crystallize into L2 outputs

PARENT — Parenting style & attachment
REL_FORM — Formative relationships
ECON_HARD — Economic hardship
DISCRIM — Discrimination experienced
COMM_NORM — Community norms
MEDIA_ENV — Media environment

Key insight: There is no 1:1 mapping. Every L2 node is a blend of multiple L1 inputs — and the same L1 node (e.g., TR) can be a primary driver for one L2 and an inhibitor for another.

High TR simultaneously amplifies T, suppresses EC, suppresses D, and suppresses RC. One dial, four downstream effects.
